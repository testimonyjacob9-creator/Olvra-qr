About Lumora

Small, useful tools for everyday life. Free to start, nothing to download.

What Lumora is

Lumora brings a handful of everyday tools into one simple app: make a QR code, design a digital business card, send someone a private animated love message, get help wording a reply in a chat, or send a gift voucher. It works in your browser and can be installed on your phone like an app.

What you can do

  • QR codesFor links, text or a WhatsApp chat. Download as PNG or SVG.
  • Business cardsPick a style, then download it or save the contact file.
  • Love messagesWrite from the heart and share one private link.
  • Chat helperPick a voice and get reply suggestions you can send.
  • Gift vouchersSend airtime, data or cash to someone you care about.

Is Lumora free?

Yes. Free accounts include a limited number of love messages. Premium removes the limit for 30 days at a time and doesn't renew automatically.

Where are you based?

Lumora is built and run from Nigeria.

How do I report a message?

Open the message link and tap "Report this message" at the bottom of the page.
