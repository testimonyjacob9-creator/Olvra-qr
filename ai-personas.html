Questions

What is Lumora AI?

Lumora AI is the name many people use for Lumora OLVRA, an AI platform and toolset from OLVRA SYSTEMS TECHNOLOGIES LTD.

Which personas are free?

Alex, The Tutor and The Witty One are free. The other seven are Premium. Premium unlocks them for 30 days at a time and does not renew automatically.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Sign in with a free account to chat. Conversations are private to your account.

Are these real people?

No. The personas are AI characters. Jane and Sherlock are original fictional characters and are not real people or actors.
