Help & Support

How Lumora works, answers to common questions, and how to reach a human.

How to use Lumora

QR code: open QR code, choose a link, text or WhatsApp chat, then download it as PNG or SVG.

Business card: open Business card, fill in your details, pick a style, and download the card or save it as a contact file.

Love message: open Love message, write it, pick an animation, then share the private link it gives you. Only someone with the link can open it.

Frequently asked questions

Is Lumora free?

QR codes and business cards are always free. Love messages are free up to a limit, then Lumora Premium removes that limit for 30 days.

Do I need an account?

Yes. A free account lets Lumora keep track of your love-message limit and your premium status.

Can I get a refund?

Contact support below with your payment reference and we'll look into it.

Someone sent me an inappropriate message. What do I do?

Open the message and tap "Report this message" at the bottom. You can also message support below.

Still stuck?

Tap the chat bubble in the corner to talk to Olives, our support assistant.
