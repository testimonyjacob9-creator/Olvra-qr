Help & Support

How Lumora works, answers to common questions, and how to reach a human.

How to use Lumora

QR code: open QR code, choose a link, text or WhatsApp chat, then download it as PNG or SVG.

Business card: open Business card, fill in your details, pick a style, and download the card or save it as a contact file.

Love message: open Love message, write it, pick an animation, then share the private link it gives you. Only someone with the link can open it.

Chat: open Chat, add a friend by the email they signed up with, and message them once they accept. Use Report inside a chat if someone behaves badly.

FAQ

Is Lumora free?

Yes, with a limit on love messages. Premium removes it for 30 days.

Why do I need an account?

So Lumora can track your free limit and premium status.

Got an inappropriate message?

Open it and tap "Report this message" at the bottom.

Still stuck? Tap the chat bubble to talk to Olives.
